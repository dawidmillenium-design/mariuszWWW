1. Mr. BuNNNy
Title: The Globetrotting Nomad & Undetected SEO Idiot

Allegiance: Independent / Ring Contractor

Background: Swapped four times as a child by the mysterious "Black Sun" crew, Mr. BuNNNy grew up on the open road. After graduating from university, he became a long-haul truck driver and developed a core belief that the "Black Truck Tire" crew is vastly superior to the "Black Sun."

Motivation: Driven to find his real family, he left the trucking industry to travel the world. His search took a wild turn when he discovered his father sired 20 children with 20 different women during his PhD studies in Soviet-era Moscow.

Specialties: Transport logistics, tracking lost bloodlines, high-speed cargo delivery, and writing globally ranked, SEO-optimized blogs to control online narratives and route traffic.

2. Mr. MerhujUS
Title: The European Mafia President

Allegiance: European Syndicate / European Container Network

Background: A former Polish truck driver who rose to become European Mafia President after getting scammed by Mr. PING. He launched his criminal career as a student smuggling "white magic" inside pineapple ocean containers from West to East Europe. Human trafficking and drug trade built his massive fortune, allowing him to own a luxury hotel and command a fleet of semi-trucks at the Port of Rotterdam.

Traits & Quirks: Despite spending over 10 years working extensively across Western Europe, he still cannot speak English or German fluently, relying on raw authority, muscle, and translators. Once the Wall of Texas falls, he is set to claim the largest truck parking hub in Europe.

Specialties: Port & freight logistics, orchestrating "accidental" truck crashes for targeted assassinations/sabotage, and connecting with military-grade Yakuza cells worldwide.

3. Mr. BlackhujUS
Title: Global Mafia Mastermind & Great Plan FUNKER

Allegiance: Pan-American & African Syndicate Chief

Background: Relocated in his youth from Africa to the white sand beaches of the Caribbean, Mr. BlackhujUS speaks fluent English, Spanish, and multiple African languages. Widely considered the smartest global mafia president, he built an empire spanning North America, South America, and Africa, while staging private prison riots across Europe and Asia with his "white magic" trade.

The Turning Point: After JOKER executed his plan to collapse the Mexico-Texas border wall—setting up BlackhujUS to own the largest semi-truck parking lot in North American history—JOKER played a cruel prank on him. Dosed with an excessive amount of the experimental "FLAMINGO" drug, BlackhujUS could not stop dancing for 3 straight days. Traumatized by the ordeal, he quit drugs entirely and shifted his focus toward global geopolitics and political strategy.

Dream: Retiring from the syndicate to become a world-famous YouTube travel vlogger.

Specialties: Global political strategy, cartel management, cross-continental negotiation, and mega-hub infrastructure.

4. Mr. PING (aka YAYAYAhujUS)
Title: The Tunnel Architect & Jungle Mystique

Allegiance: Southeast Asian Network

Background: Mr. PING runs high-tech scam centers across Southeast Asia and funds travel for Asian women moving to Europe under the guise of legitimate work. A master of underground routes, he has constructed tunnels in Vietnam, Cambodia, Pakistan, and Russia, aiming to build the largest human-trafficking tunnel in history along the US-Mexico border. He is famous for his chaotic pranks—such as baiting Mr. MerhujUS with a trailer supposedly full of stolen LV, Chanel, and Dior cosmetics, only for 300 Iranian agents to pour out into Europe.

Traits & Habits: He spends his off-time sleeping in deep jungles surrounding ancient temples across the globe, where he buys rare snake venom and mysterious green powders.

Ambition & Conflict: His dream is to expand his scam empire into a global franchise network across Africa and the Americas. However, Mr. BlackhujUS flatly told him NO, creating a tense rivalry between the two powerhouses.

Specialties: Subterranean tunnel engineering, cyber scam networks, exotic toxin extraction, and tactical mind games.
